User Guide

How to get around the site:

  • 1 Home gives an overview of the CAIT–Transit partnership and highlights the current featured project.
  • 2 Projects lists every project, each linking to its own full page.
  • 3 Resources covers site-wide documentation and links to available reports.
  • 4 Support is where to go for questions concerning the dashboard.
